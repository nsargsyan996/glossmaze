This level is solving itself. Swipe, drag or use the arrow keys to play.

How it plays

  1. Swipe

    Send the ball up, down, left or right. You can turn mid-swipe without lifting your finger.

  2. It keeps rolling

    The ball only stops when it hits a wall, so the puzzle is choosing where you'll stop.

  3. Paint every tile

    Every tile the ball rolls over gets painted. Cover the whole maze to finish the level.
